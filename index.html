What we do

Three core competencies, on top of cutting edge technology

Strategy

We work closely with executive management to set the strategic agenda and detail the initiatives that follow, from portfolio planning and market entry to operating model development and large-scale change.

Operations

Shoulder to shoulder with our clients, we optimise the cost base, raise efficiency and reduce waste across production, maintenance, quality, supply chain and procurement, and deliver large capital projects on their objectives.

M&A

We support our clients in shaping their business through acquisitions, divestments and sales processes: managing the full due diligence, valuation and vendor CDD, and the integration that follows.

AI & technology

The platform layer for all our work. We build AI and data into the analysis we do, the solutions we deliver and the operating models we leave behind, so that every engagement raises the client's own capability. Where an engagement calls for it, we bring in deep technical expertise from our Nevral team.

In strategy

  • AI strategy and operating model
  • Deep market and competitor analysis
  • Scenario and portfolio modelling

In operations

  • Predictive maintenance and quality
  • Demand and procurement optimisation
  • Automated planning and reporting

In M&A

  • Accelerated commercial due diligence
  • Data-room review and synergy modelling
  • Post-merger integration tracking
